# campaign-final: the final pass's config and recipe

The final pass (roadmap F-REPRO; user directive 2026-10-10) reruns every suite and dataset the campaign drew claims from,
at one frozen library code_version (`FINAL_TAG`), into `campaign-final/` on the Hub. Every number stays NOT CITABLE until
D1-G passes.

| file | what |
|---|---|
| [`recipe.yaml`](recipe.yaml) | the pass in one file: tag placeholders, pods, dataset pins, staging, families (one box each) and the T1 rows they feed, the 84 legs per GPU (79 grid legs, 5 C6 ceiling-check script legs) (order, `after` dependencies, env, the exact `bench` command, Hub upload path, expected cells / GPU-h), per-GPU totals, the D1-G gates. Generated by `plan.py` |
| [`plan.py`](plan.py) | the placement rules (Plan A': box = host; every family compared against V1 / V2 on pod d) and the pricing, from the sizing inventory (`docs/artifacts/f-repro/sizing.py` over the final `suites.yaml`) |
| [`ceiling.sh`](ceiling.sh) | the C6 recall-ceiling checks as script legs, one per bench (`campaign-v2.11/ceiling-int8-laion30m/ceiling_check.py`, quality only) at the points of [`ceiling-points.json`](ceiling-points.json) (the measured ones: goodreads / arXiv and their synth at the v2.10 tune's best clause point, PubMed 16384 / 4096, YFCC synth 4096 / 256, LAION 16384 / 4096); on C6's GPU after the bench's tune legs; LAION's synth p001 reads a scratch `ceil` suite, as the v2.11 driver did |
| [`build_tune.py`](build_tune.py) | writes the `tune-q` / `tune-timed` blocks of `evaluation/config/suites.yaml` from the per-bench grids and [`tune-timed-candidates.json`](tune-timed-candidates.json) (goodreads / arXiv / synth from the v2.10 quality grid, YFCC its v2.9 frontier, PubMed its v2.10 candidates; points past the n_lists / 4 cap dropped) |

## The final grid

[`evaluation/config/suites.yaml`](../../../evaluation/config/suites.yaml) holds 3,682 cells in 22 suites. The table is
[evaluation § Config](../../system/evaluation.md#config-one-yaml-per-dataset--suitesyaml), and the counts are pinned
in `evaluation/tests/bench/test_config.py` (`GRID`).

Seeds: seed 0 everywhere, with seeds 0-2 in `filter` only (T2's CIs).

**Folded in:** `c3`, `c3-real`, `codesign-pubmed`, `laion30m-x`, `c7-scorepath` (plus n_probe 32), `tune-q`, `tune-timed`. `v1v2` is covered by `synth`.

**Dropped:**
- `deep`'s V3 arm and its untuned n_lists grids (its official bloom curve stays, paired with triton: F3, C7);
- `filter`'s SilverTorch torch arms (C3 runs on V1 / V2 in `c3` / `c3-real`);
- goodreads-synth's bloom V1 / V2 and its SilverTorch / official bloom arms;
- goodreads-synth SilverTorch n_probe 128 / 512;
- `codesign` goodreads `c2_format` / `c3_year`.

**Retuned:**
- `deep` runs at the tuned n_lists, up to the n_lists / 4 cap. PubMed takes the 21 points of its v2.11 timed fronts.
- PubMed `filter` runs 4096 × {24, 512, 1024}, plus `all5` at 16384 / 2048 and `c3_journal_reverse` at 65536 / 1024.
- arxiv-synth gets nine rates for C1.
- arxiv- and corr-synth SilverTorch is capped at n_probe 512.

## Sizing

66.4 GPU-h including the measured 1.45 process overhead. Quality-only tune legs, and the timed tune legs v2.11 measured (goodreads, YFCC, PubMed), are priced from their measured runs; arXiv's timed tune is not measured yet.

| GPU | GPU-h |
|---|---|
| pod d GPU 0 | 19.8 |
| pod d GPU 1 | 19.4 |
| pod 1 | 11.2 |
| pod b | 15.9 |

So about 20 h wall, if M1 lets pod d time on both GPUs at once; otherwise pod d runs its two GPU queues in turn, about 37 h.
