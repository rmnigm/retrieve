## 3. Harness, metrics, protocol (§3)

### Q10. Recall

- **Ours**: `recall_oracle@k` = per query, |returned top-k ∩ exact filtered top-k| / k, averaged over queries; the oracle is our exact fp32 full scan under the same exact filter, top-`max(ks)`, cached by input fingerprint (blob v4) — [evaluation → measurement protocol](../../../docs/system/evaluation.md#measurement-protocol) step 4. Results-draft wording: "the share of the exact filtered top-k (the k items with the highest inner product over the whole catalogue, computed in fp32 under the same filter) that the method returns, averaged over queries" (`results.md` on dev/results-draft). Citable (definition).

### Q11. Latency

All from [evaluation → measurement protocol](../../../docs/system/evaluation.md#measurement-protocol) (step 5) and [reading the numbers](../../../docs/system/evaluation.md#reading-the-numbers). Citable (design).

- Timed region: one forward `(query batch, prepared batch)` per call; query preparation (`prepare_queries`: parsed plans, bloom signatures, attrs) runs before the first timed call and is reported as `query_prep_ms` ([query preparation](../../../docs/system/evaluation.md#query-preparation)); user rule "forward is the algorithm" (memory: no glue in timed path).
- Process setup: TF32 off, `float32_matmul_precision("highest")`, one GPU warm-up.
- Per (bs, k, mode): 50 warm-up calls → sync → **3 windows** of `N = clamp(2 s / median_est, 1000, 5000)` calls; each call bracketed by CUDA events; one sync before and after a window, none inside.
- Estimator: from the window with the median median: `median_ms` (headline), `mean_ms`, `trimmed_mean_ms`, `p95_ms`, `p99_ms`, `min_ms`, `iqr_ms`, `qps = N·bs / wall_s` (closed loop). Per-call samples go to a sidecar.
- `spread = (max − min) / median` of the 3 window medians; `spread > 0.05` → perf entry `unstable: true`.
- Modes: `graph` = `torch.compile(mode="reduce-overhead", dynamic=False, fullgraph=True)` per bs, asserts `cudagraph_skips == 0` and one `cudaGraphLaunch` per call, else null with `reason`; `eager` always. Headline: graph; eager secondary; Meta as published is eager only ([decisions campaign v2](../../decisions.md#campaign-v2-user-2026-10-08), "Timing"); the harness page's step 7 says the same since `e7119b3`.
- Inputs: fixed-seed pool of query batches rotated round-robin, identical across arms; no L2 flush (serving steady state, not cold cache).
- Batch: bs ∈ {1, 16, 64} (+256 where LiNR fits; TIMEOUT cells listed by the NUMBERS gate); per-batch latency, not per query.
- Interleaving: arms of one comparison in one process; per (bs, k, mode) each arm warms and calibrates, then window i of every arm before window i+1 (ABAB over 3 windows); graph arms captured side by side; `elapsed_s` of an interleaved record is the whole group's wall ([interleaved groups](../../../docs/system/evaluation.md#interleaved-groups)). Every ratio claim (C1, C5, C7) and SilverTorch-vs-LiNR (NUMBERS item 5) is interleaved.
- Statistics ([statistics](../../../docs/system/evaluation.md#statistics)) — **not used in this pass**: every NUMBERS and final-pass number is seed 0, so no table carries a CI; seeds and error bars come with FULL-RERUN (controller c3 ruling 7, user 2026-10-11). The machinery: percentile bootstrap, **B = 10,000**, fixed seed `stats.SEED`; one arm's latency = median of per-window medians pooled over seed × window; interleaved ratio = median of per-round ratios, resampling seed × round; otherwise median / median, resampled independently, marked ^u; a CI containing 1.0 prints **"no difference"**; no repeats added to force significance. Matched recall: `stats.at_recall`, piecewise-linear between bracketing points, never extrapolated ("not reached" / "first point already above").
- Ours vs papers: LiNR reports average + p95 (we have mean and p95 in every record); SilverTorch reports average latency (breakdown) and client QPS under a 200 ms P99 budget (end to end, 5 runs averaged, 50 warm-up + 100 test batches in the ANN breakdown) — §6 "Evaluation" / §6.2.1 of the box copy. Our closed-loop QPS is not their open-loop client QPS.
- Survey context: 0 of 11 surveyed repro papers report statistics on efficiency numbers (planner survey note in `.chains/paper-plan/`, pattern 8).
