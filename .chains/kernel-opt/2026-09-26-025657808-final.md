**Finding 8 — non-power-of-two widths (not implemented, on instruction).** The Triton kernels need a power-of-two `D` or `W` (`tl.arange`), and since Phase 2 they raise `ValueError` at the op boundary. On pubmed (D = 768) this blocks SilverTorch triton, LiNR V2 and LiNR V3 (OPORP's default k_bits = D gives W = 12); official and the cuBLAS paths (V1, V4) run. A fix would be masked padding to the next power of two in `codesigned_probe_score*`, `fused_masked_knn_topk` and OPORP, with its own parity and timing gates. `docs/roadmap.md` untouched.
