## What changed
- **`evaluation/training` and its tests:** 74 lines added, 205 deleted.
  - Gone: `HSTUBlock`, `RelativeBias`, `time_bucket`, `BLOCKS`, the `encoder` field, `hidden_dim`, `in_proj`/`out_proj`, `use_time`, `time_buckets`, and all timestamp plumbing in model, dataset, evaluate, encode and train.
  - The trainer reads only `item_ids`, plus `targets` at eval.
  - The `SASRecBlock` math is unchanged, and so is the parameter registration order.
- **Two losses:** `loss: gbce | sampled_softmax`.
  - gBCE keeps its per-position negatives and `gbce_t`.
  - `normalize` and `logq` default to `None`, which resolves to on for sampled_softmax and off for gbce. The rejections of either one with gbce stay.
- **Defaults are the E1c `config.json`:**
  - body: d64, 2 blocks, 2 heads, ffn 256, dropout 0.5;
  - loss: sampled_softmax, temperature 0.05, 8192 uniform + 4096 in-batch negatives, normalize and logQ on;
  - loop: lr 1e-3, B 256, L 200, warmup 1000, compile, 100 epochs, eval every 2, patience 10, eval_batch_size 1024, early stop on ndcg@10;
  - W&B project `seqrec-encoder`.
  - The Gate B recipe is now `loss=gbce num_negatives=256 warmup_steps=0`.
- **Legacy loading:**
  - `TrainConfig.load` reads a `config.json` with no `loss` key (the published gSASRec checkpoints) as `loss=gbce`, so `normalize` is false. It still drops unknown keys.
  - `D128_DROP05_DEFAULTS` has `loss: gbce` and no longer has `encoder`.
  - The key rename is unchanged.
  - Snapshots are named `sasrec-ep{N}-…pt`, which still matches `EPOCH_SNAPSHOT_PATTERN`.
- **Docs:**
  - datasets.md § Training: the one body, the two losses with the default, the recipe in the run examples. I also removed the stale kuairand sentence.
  - checkpoints.md: legacy loading and the snapshot name.
  - decisions.md § Sequential encoder: the gSASRec body, the two losses, HSTU tried in E2a/E2c and dropped, the six runs. It links to the validation rows and does not restate numbers.
  - validation.md: a "removed with the code" row, the W6 equivalence row, and a Gate A addendum.
  - AGENTS.md: the command comment.
