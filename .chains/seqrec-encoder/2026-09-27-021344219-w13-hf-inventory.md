0 GB even though they list 4 to 11 GB of files: every file duplicates an eval repo. So **deleting duplicates frees nothing**,
and **deleting a path frees nothing until the history is squashed** (eval-kuairand still counts 33.97 GB with only 0.54 GB
of current files). Private `used_storage` sum: **95.76 GB** (current-revision bytes: 84.09 GB). Before this session's
changes the current-revision figure was 116.03 GB. The limit is not exposed by the API; from the evidence it lies between
95.8 and ~103 GB.
