# R-k64 diagnosis: temporal drift, not capacity, cold items or the metric

Bounded (20:35-20:40 UTC GPU, evals only). Artifacts: docs/artifacts/seqrec-encoder/k64-diagnosis/.

1. **Protocol.** Test rows have 2x val's targets (median 246 vs 118). The recall@100 ceiling is 0.51
   vs 0.73 (recall divides by all targets), but ndcg@10 normalizes by min(10, n). Every history is
   capped at 200. Targets in the own history: ~0.005 %. The protocol does not explain a 5x ndcg@10
   gap.
2. **Calibration.** All-time most-popular: val 0.0012 / 0.0007, test 0.0027 / 0.0007. k64 beats it
   (19x val, 1.7x test). **The val day's most-popular list scores test 0.0314 / 0.0073, ~7x k64's
   0.0046 / 0.0016**, and higher than k64 on val. k64 restricted to train-seen targets has unchanged
   ndcg@10 (val 0.0233, test 0.0046). Cold items are not the cause; this corrects my k64 result note.
3. **Curve.** The val peak is at epoch 3. After it, train loss keeps falling and coverage@10 rises
   (0.0007 -> 0.0018) while val ndcg drifts to ~0.020. The model moves to the tail and overfits the
   train distribution; it is not a popularity collapse. 47.6 % of test targets were clicked on the val
   day, and 13.0 % only then.
4. **Context.** The A100 gSASRec d128 logged val 0.0133 at epoch 2; there is no test number and no
   published baseline.

**Mechanism:** next-day KuaiRand clicks follow what is trending. Training stops at 2022-05-05, val
is 05-06, test is 05-07, and the val day is never trained on. The model is 1 day stale on val and 2 on
test, and all-time logQ is the wrong popularity prior for a later day.

**Recommendation for k128: (c) do not run until the split changes**, so that the final model trains
on interactions through 2022-05-06, the day before the test day. For example: fold the val day into
train for the final fit, and select epochs on a user-held-out slice or an earlier day. That is an ETL
or trainer change, which is not e-runs' scope. d128 alone attacks capacity, and capacity is not the
bottleneck here: val peaks at epoch 3 and yesterday's top list beats the model on test. If the plan
is only usable embeddings for the retrieval benchmark and not recommendation quality, k64 may
already suffice. That is the user's call.

Not measured (bounded): test recall on the trending subset only; whether a train+val refit closes the
gap; the effect of lr, temperature or logQ-off on the epoch-3 peak.
